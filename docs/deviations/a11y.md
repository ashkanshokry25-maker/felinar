## How the audit is made trustworthy

A result of zero violations on 60 screens needs proof that axe saw the screens. Two checks make the rows fail closed:

1. Every axe run must pass at least one rule on the page, or the step fails as "not audited" (scenarios-a11y.mjs:92 to 93).
2. A positive control, `axeControl` (:106), adds a button with no name and an image with no alt text inside a closed shadow
   root on the page being audited, runs axe on it, removes it, and fails unless axe reports both `button-name` and
   `image-alt` as critical. It runs on the loader, the identity origin, the drop-in forum page and the social site frame
   (4 contexts per run). This proves axe is loaded, sees into the components' shadow roots, and reports a critical violation.

The project's components keep their interface in closed shadow roots. The scenarios' visitors open their pages with an init
script that makes `attachShadow` create open roots (:64), so axe can read them; the markup and styles are unchanged. This
is a test-only change, made in the test browser.

Tags: `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `best-practice` (:46), as the brief names. A serious or critical violation fails the row (:48);
moderate and minor ones are listed in the result line and in `a11y-<row>.json` in the run directory.
