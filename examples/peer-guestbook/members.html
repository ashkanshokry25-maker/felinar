Open this site through the felinar loader. You are looking at the static files directly, so messages and files are off.

Off by default

Messages and file listings are optional. They stay off until the site owner turns them on. Messages then wait for you to turn on chat, and a file is fetched only when you tap Fetch. Until then nothing here connects your browser to other visitors.

For the site owner

Turn the features on with the owner key, offline: ledger-admin cfg --chat members --files members. Chat and files need the site's own connection servers, so publish with --tracker wss://tracker.example.org --stun stun:stun.example.org:3478. The settings are advisory: honest loaders enforce them.

This loader has no members area

The loader that opened this page is older than the version with sign-in, messages and files. Update the loader to use them.
